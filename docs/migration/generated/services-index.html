02

Hosting and management

In the digital age, having a robust online presence is crucial for success. At Digitally Shifted, we understand this better than anyone. That’s why we offer top-tier domain registration and hosting services designed to empower your digital aspirations—whether you’re a budding entrepreneur, a growing business, or an established brand looking to enhance your online footprint.

Domains

A name you can trust

Finding the perfect domain name is the first step in establishing a strong online identity. Our domain service provides you with access to a wide range of domain extensions (from classic .com and .net to niche-specific like .tech or .shop), ensuring that you can find the domain that best fits your business’s personality and mission.

Protection and privacy

We prioritize your privacy with features designed to protect your personal information from being exposed in the WHOIS database. Our domain privacy options shield your data, reducing the risk of spam and identity theft.

Hosting

Fast and reliable dedicated servers

Our hosting is powered by dedicated servers optimized for speed and reliability, not using the traditional slow Apache setup but the cutting-edge LiteSpeed technology. This ensures exceptionally low Time to First Byte (TTFB) and lightning-fast page loading times, thanks to advanced caching mechanisms inherent to LiteSpeed.

Scalable solutions

Whether you’re just starting out or need to handle increasing traffic, our hosting plans grow with your business. Upgrade your resources with just a few clicks to ensure that your website maintains peak performance during critical times.

Enhanced security

Your site’s security is paramount. Our servers are fortified with Imunify360, providing a robust security framework against digital threats. This high-security setup actively protects your website from malware, unauthorized access, and other vulnerabilities.

SpamExperts for email security

We use SpamExperts to ensure that your emails remain clear of spam and phishing attacks. This top-of-the-line solution filters out harmful and unwanted content before it reaches your inbox, enhancing communication security and efficiency.

Expert support

Our experienced support team is here to help you every step of the way. Whether you need assistance with setting up your domain, managing your hosting environment, or troubleshooting, we’re just a message away, available 24/7.
